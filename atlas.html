About this atlas

New York City's street-lighting maps, shown large above, are the originals. The four smaller maps — Chicago, Philadelphia, Baltimore and Los Angeles — adapt the same method to each city's open data. Click any mini map to load it into the main view and explore it; click "open" to view it on its own.

Darkness × crime crosses calibrated nighttime satellite radiance (the Visible Infrared Imaging Radiometer Suite, VIIRS, Black Marble composite) with violent crime. 311 × crime crosses resident-reported streetlight-outage complaints with violent crime. Within each city map the two layers share the same time window.

The comparison-city maps are drafts for review, not published work.
