What's being measured

Nighttime only. Only incidents from 8 PM to 6 AM (by the incident timestamp) are counted — darkness is a nighttime condition, so daytime crime is excluded.

Geography. H3 hexagons at resolution — roughly a few blocks apiece. Cells with no events are not drawn.

How "high" is defined

"Story" cells (high on both): hexes.

Caveats

311 reporting bias. Complaint volume tracks who calls 311 as much as actual outage incidence; under-reporting in some neighborhoods is real.
